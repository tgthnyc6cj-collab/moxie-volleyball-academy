THE HEART BEHIND MOXIE

For Coach JoJo, volleyball has always been about more than the game: the relationships built, confidence gained, lessons learned, and the moment an athlete realizes what they can achieve.

Jodee Abercrombie has coached boys’ and girls’ competitive volleyball and is passionate about developing athletes’ skills, character, leadership, and self-belief. She currently serves as a Coach and Program Assistant for Basha High School Girls Volleyball and Head Coach of Maverick Volleyball Academy. She holds USA Volleyball and NFHS certifications.

WHY SHE CREATED MOXIE

Coach JoJo envisioned an academy where athletes are never just filling roster spots. Development, communication, accountability, and meaningful relationships are as important as competition. Families deserve transparency, consistency, and confidence in their program.

MORE THAN VOLLEYBALL

MOXIE focuses on quality training, competitive opportunities, strong team culture, and mental performance development. Athletes learn to respond to adversity, be accountable, compete with purpose, and support their teammates.

“I want our athletes to leave each season knowing they were valued, challenged, supported, and genuinely cared for.”

— Coach JoJo · Founder & Director
